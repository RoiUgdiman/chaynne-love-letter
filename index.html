Today is all about celebrating you. ❤️

Happy birthday, my love! ❤️ Gusto lang gyud nako nga makabalo ka kung unsa ka ka important sa akoa. I want you to feel nga naa kay someone nga willing maminaw nimo, understand you, and stay beside you even when things are not okay. I want to make you happy, not just with words, but with the little things that show you how much I care. You have given me so many memories, so much happiness, and a kind of love that I will always be thankful for. If I could choose one thing to ask for, it would be more time with you. More mornings, more nights, more random conversations, more walks, more dates, more laughs, more quiet moments, and more days where I get to call you mine. I do not know what the future will look like, but I know that I want to keep choosing you and making you feel loved. You mean more to me than I can explain, Chaynne. Thank you for staying, for loving me, and for being you. I love you so much, my love. Happy birthday. ❤️

Love always, Roi ❤️
